# Module 06: Acoustic Fine-Tuning, Scaling & Experimental Optimization

## Module Description
Transfer learning, AdamW, cosine annealing, mixed precision (BF16), acoustic data scaling (B-01 to B-02C), and debugging training failures.

## Primary Research Connection
Stage 9 (B-02A/B/C scaling campaign) and Stage 12/13 (Acoustic regularization).

## Lectures in this Module

- **[LECTURE_18](../../lectures/lecture_18/README.md)**: Fine-Tuning Pretrained Models: Schedulers, Mixed Precision, and Regularization
- **[LECTURE_19](../../lectures/lecture_19/README.md)**: Acoustic Data Scaling: The Empirical Trajectory from B-01 to B-02C
- **[LECTURE_20](../../lectures/lecture_20/README.md)**: Debugging ASR Systems: NaNs, Exploding Gradients, and Memory Profiling

## Key Learning Outcomes
By completing this module, students will:
1. Master the theoretical mathematics and engineering principles of this stage of the ASR pipeline.
2. Understand the exact historical decisions, failures, and empirical breakthroughs achieved in the Kashmiri AI initiative.
3. Implement, debug, and run corresponding modular code on authentic speech datasets.
