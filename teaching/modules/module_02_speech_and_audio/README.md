# Module 02: Speech Signal Processing & The Mathematics of Sound

## Module Description
Waveform physics, Nyquist sampling theorem, PCM encoding, windowed sinc resampling, Fourier transforms, spectrograms, and filterbanks.

## Primary Research Connection
Stage 20R/20S (22.05 kHz audio bug discovery and Kaiser windowed sinc recovery).

## Lectures in this Module

- **[LECTURE_03](../../lectures/lecture_03/README.md)**: The Mathematics of Sound and Digital Audio
- **[LECTURE_04](../../lectures/lecture_04/README.md)**: Audio Preprocessing, Resampling, and the 22.05 kHz Defect
- **[LECTURE_05](../../lectures/lecture_05/README.md)**: Fourier Transforms, Spectrograms, and Filterbanks

## Key Learning Outcomes
By completing this module, students will:
1. Master the theoretical mathematics and engineering principles of this stage of the ASR pipeline.
2. Understand the exact historical decisions, failures, and empirical breakthroughs achieved in the Kashmiri AI initiative.
3. Implement, debug, and run corresponding modular code on authentic speech datasets.
