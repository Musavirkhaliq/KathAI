Shivaratri and
Id
Several festivals are celebrated in Kashmir.
Shivratri and Id is special among them. Shivratri is called �herath� in
Kashmiri. Herath is a festival of Hindus. Before Herath, the homes are cleaned
and broomed. On that day �VaTukh� is bought and worshiped. Next day is
called Salam.
