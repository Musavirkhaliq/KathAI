## 1. Executive Summary

The EduCasheer Kashmiri ASR Corpus encompasses **139,426 human speech clips** (368.01 hours) across **641 distinct speakers**, representing all 10 administrative districts of the Kashmir Valley plus standard literary and religious registers.

## 3. Dialect Classification & Phonetic Variants

The corpus systematically captures the three major regional dialect groups of Kashmiri:

1. **Maraz (Southern Kashmiri)**: Characterized by distinctive vowel shifts and verbal inflection, prevalent in Anantnag, Pulwama, Kulgam, and Shopian.
2. **Kamraz (Northern Kashmiri)**: Characterized by phonetic variations in dental sibilants and archaic lexical forms, prevalent in Baramulla, Kupwara, and Bandipora.
3. **Yamraz / Central Kashmiri (Standard Srinagar)**: The prestige dialect spoken in Srinagar, Budgam, and Ganderbal, which serves as the primary standard for orthography and media.
