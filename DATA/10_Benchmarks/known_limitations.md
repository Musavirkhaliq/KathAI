# Known Benchmark Limitations

1. **Dialect Balance**: Tier A is heavily concentrated in Srinagar, Anantnag, and Baramulla; border dialects (Kupwara Karnah, Gurez Shina-influenced Kashmiri) require further representation.
2. **Acoustic Noise Diversity**: Tier C features studio-clean recordings (28.4 dB SNR), whereas Tier B represents mobile environments (12-16 dB SNR).
