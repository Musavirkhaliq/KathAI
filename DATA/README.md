# DATA: Canonical Kashmiri AI Research & Documentation Suite

Welcome to the definitive research and documentation directory for the EduCasheer Kashmiri AI Research Project. This directory provides an independently audited, evidence-backed reference for all data assets, acquisition pipelines, benchmarks, and training histories.

## Directory Sitemap

- **[01_Master_Inventory/](file:///home/musa/Kashmiri%20Project/DATA/01_Master_Inventory/)**: Physical file parquet inventory (256,496 files), CSV/JSON summaries, and storage analysis.
- **[02_Data_Collection/](file:///home/musa/Kashmiri%20Project/DATA/02_Data_Collection/)**: Provenance and acquisition histories of external, Quranic, and WAAZ community collections.
- **[03_Speech_Data/](file:///home/musa/Kashmiri%20Project/DATA/03_Speech_Data/)**: Comprehensive audio statistics across 226,487 recordings (542.28 physical hours).
- **[04_Text_Data/](file:///home/musa/Kashmiri%20Project/DATA/04_Text_Data/)**: Text corpus statistics across 3.6 GB of monolingual and instruction text.
- **[05_Translation_Data/](file:///home/musa/Kashmiri%20Project/DATA/05_Translation_Data/)**: Parallel machine translation corpus (344,445 sentence pairs).
- **[06_Linguistic_Resources/](file:///home/musa/Kashmiri%20Project/DATA/06_Linguistic_Resources/)**: Dictionaries and lexicons (131,231 entries, 29,472 unique words).
- **[07_Data_Processing/](file:///home/musa/Kashmiri%20Project/DATA/07_Data_Processing/)**: Universal audio standardization, VAD, and normalization specifications.
- **[08_Deduplication_and_Lineage/](file:///home/musa/Kashmiri%20Project/DATA/08_Deduplication_and_Lineage/)**: Duplicate audits, lineage graphs, and acoustic collision deconfliction.
- **[09_Training_Data_History/](file:///home/musa/Kashmiri%20Project/DATA/09_Training_Data_History/)**: Model exposure mapping across B-01, B-02, Stage 12, Stage 22, and Stage 29.
- **[10_Benchmarks/](file:///home/musa/Kashmiri%20Project/DATA/10_Benchmarks/)**: Forensic audit of Tier A, B, C benchmarks and zero-contamination verification.
- **[11_Data_Readiness/](file:///home/musa/Kashmiri%20Project/DATA/11_Data_Readiness/)**: Unified readiness matrix (CSV, JSON, Parquet) and tiered manifests.
- **[12_Licensing_and_Ethics/](file:///home/musa/Kashmiri%20Project/DATA/12_Licensing_and_Ethics/)**: Legal compliance registry, community consent, and redistribution terms.
- **[13_Research_Paper/](file:///home/musa/Kashmiri%20Project/DATA/13_Research_Paper/)**: Publication-ready manuscript sections, LaTeX tables, and BibTeX citations.
- **[14_Visualizations/](file:///home/musa/Kashmiri%20Project/DATA/14_Visualizations/)**: 10 publication figures in high-res PNG and SVG formats.
- **[15_Audit_Code/](file:///home/musa/Kashmiri%20Project/DATA/15_Audit_Code/)**: Reproducible Python audit and verification scripts.
- **[16_Audit_Evidence/](file:///home/musa/Kashmiri%20Project/DATA/16_Audit_Evidence/)**: Execution logs, discrepancy reports, and checksum validation artifacts.
- **[17_Future_Data_Strategy/](file:///home/musa/Kashmiri%20Project/DATA/17_Future_Data_Strategy/)**: Immediate, short-term, and long-term data expansion roadmaps.
