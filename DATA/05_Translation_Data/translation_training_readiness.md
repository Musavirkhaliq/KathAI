# Machine Translation Training Readiness

- **English -> Kashmiri**: Over 150k verified sentence pairs ready for fine-tuning IndicTrans2, NLLB-200, or LLaMA-based MT models.
- **Urdu -> Kashmiri**: High syntactic similarity; useful for transfer learning and low-resource adaptation.
- **Reverse Direction Duplication**: Many Kashmiri -> English entries represent inverse mappings of the primary English -> Kashmiri corpus; bidirectional deduplication should be applied during MT training.
