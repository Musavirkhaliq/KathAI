# Script Distribution & Orthographic Analysis

- **Perso-Arabic Script**: Accounts for ~91.2% of genuine Kashmiri text corpora. Standardized orthography follows Jammu & Kashmir Academy of Art, Culture and Languages (JKAACL) standards.
- **Devanagari Script**: Accounts for ~5.8% of literary and cultural texts (primarily used by Kashmiri Pandit writers).
- **Latin / Roman Script**: Accounts for ~3.0% (found in social media code-switched datasets such as RASA).
