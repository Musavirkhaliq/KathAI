# Analysis of Unused and Recoverable Kashmiri Speech Data

Of our 542.28 physical hours of Kashmiri human speech, **363.68 hours (67.1%)** have never been exposed to any acoustic model training pipeline.

### Unused Data Categories:
1. **Gold Tier Unused (2.86 hours)**: 100% verified verbatim speech, ready for direct supervised training.
2. **Silver Tier (151.41 hours)**: Al Fajr complete Quran (62.03h), Molvi Salafi 30 Paras (41.87h), Mirwaiz pure commentary (23.25h), language courses (5.22h), Omarrran single speaker (4.42h).
3. **Bronze Tier (5.82 hours)**: WAAZ review queue (3.80h) and Akashvani news broadcasts (2.02h).
4. **Unsegmented Long-Form Audio (174.28 hours)**: Unsegmented raw broadcasts and archive reels ready for VAD segmentation.
