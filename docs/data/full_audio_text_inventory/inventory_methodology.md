## 1. Scientific Standards & Constraints

In strict conformance with the Master Task requirements:
- **Zero Destructive Actions**: No files were moved, renamed, deleted, or overwritten. Original manifests and archives were preserved unchanged.
- **Zero Unsanctioned Downloading**: No external datasets were downloaded.
- **Zero Training Execution**: No GPUs were allocated to training loops or expensive gradient runs.
- **Decoded Ground Truth Measurement**: Audio durations were computed from physical headers (RIFF/WAV sample counts, MP3 frames) rather than relying on nominal manifest fields.
- **Streaming & Metadata-First Inspection**: Massive Parquet files and compressed tar archives were indexed using schema headers without loading gigabytes of raw binary waveforms into RAM.

## 3. Step-by-Step Reproduction Workflow

The audit pipeline is encapsulated in `src/data_audit/`. To reproduce the entire audit from scratch, execute:

### Step 1: Physical Audio Discovery & Scan
```bash
python3 src/data_audit/01_scan_physical_audio.py
```
- **Operations**: Traverses `kashmiri_dataset/processed/audio`, `raw`, `Data/`, and `DataCollection/data`. Resolves symlinks, reads header sample counts and sampling rates via `soundfile.info` and `mutagen.mp3`.
- **Throughput**: ~3,000 files/second.
- **Output Artifact**: `src/data_audit/audit_physical_audio.parquet` (220,747 entries; 174,766 physical standalone files).

### Step 2: Transcript & Manifest Extraction
```bash
python3 src/data_audit/02_audit_transcripts_and_manifests.py
```
- **Operations**: Ingests master manifest, source JSONLs, DataCollection SQLite tables (`recordings`, `transcripts`, `quality_assessments`), and OpenSLR tar manifests. Evaluates Unicode scripts, detects Perso-Arabic diacritics, and assigns preliminary alignment tags.
- **Output Artifact**: `src/data_audit/audit_manifest_pairs.parquet` (238,843 candidate text records).

### Step 3: Training & Benchmark Cross-Audit
```bash
python3 src/data_audit/03_cross_audit_training_benchmarks.py
```
- **Operations**: Cross-references physical audio against frozen benchmarks (canonical test/val, Stage 9 test/val, Dev B WAAZ, Omnilingual test). Measures exact audio and speaker leakage. Computes overlap with historical training models (B-01, B-02A/B/C, E22-01..E22-03).
- **Output Artifact**: `docs/data/full_audio_text_inventory/training_usage_matrix.csv`.

### Step 4: Unified Registry & Deliverable Generation
```bash
python3 src/data_audit/04_build_unified_registry.py
```
- **Operations**: Merges physical media and deduplicated transcripts. Assigns final alignment categories (A through I) and eligibility tags. Generates machine-readable Parquet and CSV deliverable tables.
- **Output Artifacts**:
  - `docs/data/full_audio_text_inventory/audio_text_pairs.parquet`
  - `docs/data/full_audio_text_inventory/MASTER_AUDIO_TEXT_INVENTORY.csv`
  - `docs/data/full_audio_text_inventory/source_registry.csv`
  - `docs/data/full_audio_text_inventory/source_summary.csv`
  - `docs/data/full_audio_text_inventory/source_overlap_matrix.csv`
  - `docs/data/full_audio_text_inventory/new_data_beyond_e22_01.csv`
