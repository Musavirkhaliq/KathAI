\subsection{Kashmiri Character CTC Inventory}
The 159-token inventory comprises:
\begin{itemize}
    \item 39 Standard Perso-Arabic consonants and ligatures.
    \item 7 Contrastive central vowel diacritics: \texttt{U+0672} (\textit{Alef with Wavy Hamza Above}), \texttt{U+0620} (\textit{Kashmiri Yeh}), \texttt{U+06C6} (\textit{Waaw with V}), \texttt{U+06C4} (\textit{Waaw with circle}), \texttt{U+06CE} (\textit{Yeh with tail}), \texttt{U+06CD} (\textit{Yeh with ring}), \texttt{U+06FD} (\textit{Yeh with inverted V}).
    \item CTC Blank token (\texttt{[pad]}, index 0) and Word Boundary (\texttt{|}, index 1).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Canonical KenLM 4-Gram Specification}
The language model was trained on 1.2M cleaned Kashmiri sentences compiled from Wikipedia, Aya Kashmiri, BPCC, and translated religious corpora:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Order: 4-gram with modified Kneser-Ney smoothing.
    \item Vocabulary: 64,800 unique word types.
    \item Memory Size: 184 MB in ARPA format.
    \item Pruning: Singletons pruned at orders 3 and 4.
\end{itemize}
