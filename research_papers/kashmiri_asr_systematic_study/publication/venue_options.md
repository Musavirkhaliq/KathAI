# Target Publication Venue Options

This document analyzes prospective academic publication venues across speech technology, computational linguistics, and machine learning.

---

## 1. Primary Conference Options

### Option 1: Interspeech (International Speech Communication Association - ISCA)
- **Fit**: **EXCELLENT (Rank 1 Recommendation)**
- **Track**: Speech Recognition: Low-Resource & Multilingual / Speech Resource Curation.
- **Format**: 4 pages content + unlimited references.
- **Strengths**: Interspeech deeply values acoustic modeling, speech corpus curation, and evaluation reproducibility. The sample-rate forensic audit and power-law scaling narrative strongly resonate with ISCA reviewers.
- **Next Submission Cycle**: Early 2027 (Paper deadline: ~March 2027).

### Option 2: IEEE ICASSP (International Conference on Acoustics, Speech and Signal Processing)
- **Fit**: **HIGH**
- **Track**: Speech and Language Processing / Automatic Speech Recognition.
- **Format**: 4 pages + 1 page references.
- **Strengths**: Strong emphasis on signal processing integrity and rigorous acoustic feature extraction.
- **Next Submission Cycle**: Late 2027 (Paper deadline: ~September 2027).

### Option 3: ACL / EMNLP / COLING (Computational Linguistics & NLP)
- **Fit**: **VERY HIGH (Theme: Low-Resource & Language Resources)**
- **Track**: Phonology, Morphology, and Word Segmentation / Language Resources and Evaluation.
- **Format**: 8 pages long paper + unlimited references.
- **Strengths**: Allows expansive discussion of Kashmiri orthography, diacritic minimal pairs, and tokenizer fertility.
- **Next Submission Cycle**: ACL Rolling Review (ARR) monthly cycles.

## 2. Archival Journal Options

### Option 4: IEEE/ACM Transactions on Audio, Speech, and Language Processing (TASLP)
- **Fit**: **EXCEPTIONAL FOR EXTENDED MASTER VERSION**
- **Format**: Full journal paper (10–14 double-column IEEE format pages).
- **Strengths**: Comprehensive scope accommodates all 21 research stages, full negative results, complete error taxonomies, and exhaustive appendices without page length constraints.

### Option 5: Computer Speech & Language (Elsevier)
- **Fit**: **HIGH**
- **Focus**: Corpus design, linguistic analysis, and reproducible empirical speech recognition.
